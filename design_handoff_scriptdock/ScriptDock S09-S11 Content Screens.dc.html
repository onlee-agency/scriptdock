Site Files

Small text files that live at the root of your domain. Ad networks, security researchers and AI crawlers read them. ScriptDock serves them for you, so you never touch the server.

WordPress is installed in a subfolder. These files must sit at lumencoffee.com/ads.txt, not /blog/ads.txt. Add a redirect at the server, or place the files there by hand. How to fix this
